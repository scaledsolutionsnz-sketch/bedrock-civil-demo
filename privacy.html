Privacy Policy

Placeholder privacy policy for the Bedrock Civil website. Final wording to be confirmed with the business.

1. What we collect

This website does not use contact forms. If you call or email Bedrock Civil, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address and information about your site or project.

2. How we use it

Contact details are used to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, arrange site visits and carry out the work you engage us for. We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for marketing.

3. Third-party services

This site embeds a Google Map and loads fonts and images from third-party providers. Those providers may collect standard technical data, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

4. How long we keep it

Enquiry and job records are kept only as long as needed for quoting, delivering the work and meeting record-keeping obligations.

5. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you and request that it be corrected or deleted.
