Paraparaumu based

Ground you can build on, from a local you can ring

Bedrock Civil is run by Art out of Paraparaumu. You deal with the person who quotes the job, drives the digger and answers the phone when something changes.

Coastal sections have their own habits: sand that moves, water that finds the low point, access that gets tight. We plan for that before the first bucket goes in, so the job finishes when and where the quote said it would.
