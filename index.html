FAQ

Before you call

Still unsure? Ring Art and ask — it costs nothing.

What areas do you cover?

Bedrock Civil is based in Paraparaumu and works across the Kāpiti Coast. Outside the area? Give Art a call and ask — it depends on the job.

How do I get a quote?

Call or email with the address and what you need done. Art will come and look at the site, then give you a clear price before any work starts.

Do I need a consent for earthworks?

Some jobs do, depending on the scale and your site. Mention what you're planning when you call and Art can tell you what applies.

What should I do before work starts?

Mark out any known underground services (power, water, septic) and make sure there's access for machinery. Art will confirm the details with you before day one.

What happens after I accept the quote?

You lock in a start date, confirm access, and Art keeps you in the loop through the job — you're dealing with the same person the whole way.
